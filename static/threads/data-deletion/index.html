Tudouge Social Threads

Data Deletion Instructions — Last updated June 22, 2026

Option 1: Revoke Access

  1. Open the Threads app or website
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Website permissions
  3. Remove Tudouge Social Threads

Or remove the app from Meta Apps and Websites.

Option 2: Email Request

Email paradise.lsj@gmail.com with:

  • Subject: Data Deletion Request — Tudouge Social Threads
  • Your Threads username
  • Confirmation that you want all app-associated data deleted

We will process your request within 30 days.

What We Delete

  • Stored access tokens and session data
  • Cached posts, drafts, or retrieved content
  • Logs tied to your account in our systems

Content already published on Threads must be deleted through Threads directly.
