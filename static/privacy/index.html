Service

Scope

This policy applies only to Tudouge Social Threads, a personal tool that connects to the Threads API via Meta. It does not cover other products on anotherbug.com.

Information We Collect

When you authorize the app, we may access:

  • Your Threads profile (username, user ID)
  • Posts and replies you authorize us to read or publish
  • Public content retrieved through the Threads API at your request

We do not collect passwords. Authentication is handled by Meta.

How We Use Information

  • Read and publish Threads content when you request it
  • Operate social content management features you enable
  • Maintain and improve app functionality

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We only share data when required by law, with infrastructure providers under confidentiality obligations, or when you direct us to publish content to Threads.

Retention & Deletion

We retain data only as long as needed to provide the service. See Data Deletion Instructions. You may revoke access anytime via Threads settings or Meta authorized apps.
